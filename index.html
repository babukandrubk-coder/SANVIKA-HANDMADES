Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Detail by Detail

Sanvika Handmades began as a small labour of love — turning ribbons, beads and thread into accessories that feel personal. Every hairpin, rakhi and bracelet is designed and assembled by hand, in small batches, so that no two pieces are ever quite the same.

We believe handmade means more than beautiful — it means thoughtful. From festival gifting to everyday wear, our pieces are made to be treasured.
